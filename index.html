Style tips

Start with one bold piece

Let one colour lead and keep the rest calm. A green top with cream or white trousers always works.

Try before you buy

Put a top and bottom together in the studio first. It is the quickest way to see if two pieces belong together.

Build outfits you can repeat

Save your favourites to the almirah, then mix them across the week instead of buying new every time.

Our story

Try Clothy started with a simple problem: clothes that look perfect on a hanger and wrong once they are home. We wanted shopping to feel more like standing in front of a mirror.

So we built a store where the mirror comes first. You see the piece on you, save what you like, and decide without the guesswork.

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No. Your almirah is saved on your own device, so you can start right away.

Which photo works best?

A tall, full-body photo taken straight on against a plain wall gives the cleanest preview.

Can I try a top and trousers together?

Yes. Pick one top and one bottom and both appear on the photo. A dress replaces both.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Browsing, trying on and saving to your almirah cost nothing.

Studio questions

Why does a garment sit slightly off?

The preview places clothes by body proportions. Nudge it with the Size and Move sliders.

Can I try two tops together?

One top and one bottom at a time. Choosing a new top replaces the old one.

Where does the downloaded look go?

It saves as a PNG called try-clothy-look in your downloads folder.

Size guide

An approximate guide in inches. Measure yourself and compare, as fits vary by style.

Size Chest Waist Hip
XS 32–34 26–28 34–36
S 35–37 29–31 37–39
M 38–40 32–34 40–42
L 41–43 35–37 43–45
XL 44–46 38–40 46–48

How to measure

Chest

Wrap the tape around the fullest part, under your arms, keeping it level.

Waist

Measure at your natural waist, the narrowest part of your torso.

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of your hips with feet together.

FAQs

Is the try-on a real AI?

It is a preview that fits garments onto your photo. It is not a full AI body-reshaping model.

Are my photos stored?

No. They stay in your browser.

Can I add my own clothes?

Yes, in the Almirah page or by uploading a transparent PNG in Try-On.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, every page is responsive.

Which photo size is best?

A tall portrait photo, head to feet, works best.

How do I remove an item?

Open it in the almirah and press Remove.

Can I add my own colours?

Upload a photo of the garment in any colour and it joins your rail.
